Fulfillment Protocol

Product Fulfillment & Quality Control

Who actually builds the software, websites, and runs the client ad campaigns? expand_more
All product fulfillment is executed 100% in-house by PHOYEV's central team of full-time software engineers, video editors, and senior media buyers. As a partner, you never have to code, manage server infrastructure, or deal with technical customer support. You curate distribution and relationships; our infrastructure delivers the artifacts.
How does a customer access their software or service once purchased? expand_more
Once an order is processed through your partner referral link, automated onboarding triggers immediately. The customer receives instant portal credentials, and our onboarding team schedules a kick-off call for service retainers. Real-time telemetry lets you track client deployment milestones directly in your partner console.
What happens if a client requests custom modifications or technical revisions? expand_more
Our dedicated PHOYEV client success team handles all revisions, bug fixes, and client communications within defined SLAs. Every client contract guarantees round-the-clock priority ticketing, relieving independent partners of operational friction.
02
Financial Engineering

Payouts, Commissions & Overrides

When and how are partner commissions paid out? expand_more
Commissions are calculated bi-weekly and paid on the 1st and 15th of every month via direct bank transfer, Stripe Connect, or international wire (Wise). All verified earnings are visible in real-time in your Partner Portal with cryptographic accounting ledgers.
Are commissions one-time or recurring? expand_more
Both. Software suite subscriptions and marketing retainers pay recurring monthly commissions for the lifetime of the active client. Website builds and bootcamp enrollments pay immediate high-ticket one-time bounties upon checkout, providing an optimal blend of upfront liquidity and recurring runway.
Is there a minimum payout threshold? expand_more
The minimum payout threshold is $50. If your accrued balance is less, it rolls over automatically to the subsequent pay cycle without penalty or ledger expiration.
03
Ecosystem Ramp

Partner Training, Support & Community

What training is provided to new partners with zero sales or tech background? expand_more
Every new partner gets immediate access to the Partner Quickstart Sprint, pre-recorded pitch scripts, high-converting slide decks, email swipe files, and weekly live coaching clinics with top leaders. You also get full interactive sandbox licenses to all digital products.
Do I receive personal 1-on-1 mentorship? expand_more
Pro Network Partners and Executive Leaders are matched with an experienced mentor and gain direct access to our 24/7 Slack and Discord partner community channels, where real-time proposal reviews and tactical advice occur daily.
04
Flexibility Guarantee

Membership Upgrades, Cancellation & Refunds

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my partner membership at any time? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. You can modify or cancel your subscription at any time directly through your billing portal with zero lock-in contracts or penalty fees. Your account remains active until the end of your billing cycle.
Do you offer a refund policy? expand_more
Yes. We provide an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee on all partner membership tiers. If you decide the platform is not a fit, request a full refund within 30 days of enrollment, processed with zero friction.
05
Audit & Regulatory Mandates

Compliance, Legal & Income Disclosures

Is PHOYEV Network an MLM or pyramid scheme? expand_more
No. PHOYEV Network operates a compliant affiliate and multi-tier distribution model that adheres strictly to FTC guidelines and global direct-selling regulations. Unlike pyramid structures, our business is primarily driven by real retail software and service sales to third-party commercial clients. Recruitment alone generates zero revenue; commissions are paid exclusively upon genuine product fulfillment.
Are there any guaranteed income levels? expand_more
Absolutely not. We strictly forbid any guaranteed income promises. Partner earnings depend entirely on individual efforts, market conditions, and sales execution. Review our published Income Disclosure Statement for detailed statistics across all partner tiers.
