Statutory & Judicial Basis

In re Amway Corp., 93 F.T.C. 618 (1979) Core Rule

Established the "70% Rule": Distributors must demonstrate that at least 70% of total wholesale volume purchased is sold to end-user retail customers before receiving upline bonuses.

FTC v. Omnitrition Int'l, 79 F.3d 776 (9th Cir. 1996) Strict Proof

Affirmed that formal rules on paper are ineffective unless strictly monitored and enforced via real transaction records and verified customer receipts.

FTC v. Herbalife (2016) / Vemma (2015) Modern Standard

Mandates two-track segregation between business participants and preferred retail consumers, barring compensation driven predominantly by recruit internal consumption.
