Direct commissions and team overrides are calculated in real time inside your partner dashboard and paid out bi-weekly via direct bank transfer, Wise, or Stripe Connect in 110+ countries.

What training is included for new partners? expand_more

Every partner receives immediate access to our step-by-step onboarding academy, B2B outreach scripts, demo walkthroughs, and live weekly mentorship sessions.

Can I upgrade or cancel my partner tier anytime? expand_more

Yes. There are no long-term lock-in contracts. You can upgrade your tier as your organization grows or cancel anytime directly from your billing settings.
