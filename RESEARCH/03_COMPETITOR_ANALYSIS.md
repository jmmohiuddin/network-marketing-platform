## 0. Summary

- **All 14 names in the brief were checked.** Nine are real software vendors in or near this category, one is a direct-selling brand (Plexus, a customer type, not a competitor), one is unrelated to MLM (eLoyalty, now Mattersight), and three are unresolved (Prodigy, ARM MLM Software at listing level, and "Salesforce-based MLM solutions" as a named product).
- **At least 16 distinct MLM-software vendors exist at verification level V1** (listed in an independent directory or news); that meets the "10 vendors" part of gate G1 **at the "exists" level**. "Verified active" (recent dated evidence) is only partly established and is a Wave 2 task.
- **The two best-known enterprise names are now under one owner.** Exigo acquired DirectScale (reported 31 Oct 2022, S-061). [VF at Grade B]

## 6. Patterns and what they imply

| Pattern | Evidence | Label | Implication [SR / HY] |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long-tenured incumbents** (1978, 1987, 1998 dates) | S-067, S-069, S-071 | [IP] | Customers may have high switching costs; entrants need a migration story. **Hypothesis:** the incumbents' age creates modernization gaps; unverified. |
| **Private-equity consolidation** (Gauge/Exigo/DirectScale) | S-061, S-062 | [RB] (two B/C sources) | The enterprise tier is concentrating; consolidation can unsettle customers, and can also lead to price rises. Both [HY]. |
| **Low-end vendors compete on listing price** ($499 and below) | S-071, S-073 | [IP] (directory) | A new entrant at a low price faces many similar vendors; differentiation would have to be compliance, transparency or modern UX. [HY] |
| **Vendor-authored comparisons dominate search results** | S-072, S-078, S-008 | [IP] | Independent evidence is scarce: customer interviews and complaint mining are required |
| **Vendors market compliance features** | Not verified in depth | [UV] | Wave 2: check which vendors publish compliance or audit-trail features |
| **Many developer-shop vendors appear to be South Asia–linked** | Master Plan hypothesis; partially consistent with the listing mix | [HY] | Test by checking company locations in Wave 2 |
