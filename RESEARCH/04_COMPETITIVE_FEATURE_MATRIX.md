## 4. Observations [IP / HY / SR]

1. **Most-stated capabilities [IP]:** multi-country claims (5 of 8), commissions, back office and payout-through-provider (4 each), e-commerce, analytics and AI (3 each). A published API is stated by 2 vendors on official pages and implied for 3 more by weaker sources. This is a **candidate table-stakes list [HY]**, to be tested in interviews; it is derived from marketing pages, where silence is weak evidence.
2. **AI is mentioned by three vendors without any capability, data or accuracy statement [IP].** This is consistent with the Master Plan's challenge C5 (AI needs a measured problem). It is **not** evidence that AI features lack value.
3. **Candidate whitespace [HY, to be validated]:** (a) published pricing (1 of 8), (b) MLM-specific compliance evidence tooling (0 of 8), (c) a stated data-residency option (0 of 8). Each is a hypothesis in 05, with its validation step.
4. **Hosting and delivery model differ [IP]:** a one-time licence with white-label source (Infinite, Epixel) versus enterprise platforms-as-a-service (Exigo). Customers in these groups likely have different needs and budgets (02 §5.2).
5. **Genealogy, CRM and mobile-app capability appear on one or two pages each [IP, weak].** Do not conclude that other vendors lack them.
