**Reading rule for this document.** Everything a vendor says about itself is a **vendor claim** (Grade C), however specific. "Not stated on the page read" means only that: the page does not say it. It does **not** mean the vendor lacks the feature. Directory prices are summaries by the directory, not offers.

## 0. Summary

1. **All 14 names in the brief were checked.** Nine are real software vendors in or near this category. Plexus is a direct-selling brand (a customer type), eLoyalty is unrelated to MLM, and "Salesforce-based MLM solutions" is not a named product: no AppExchange listing was found. **ARM MLM Software is now resolved** (listed on Capterra, GetApp, TrustRadius, SourceForge and Software Advice, S-101). **"Prodigy" has a possible match, Prodigix MLM (G2, S-104), unconfirmed**; the founder should confirm (OQ-05).
2. **Only four vendors have strong dated evidence of 2026 activity** (a vendor page modified or authored in 2026, or reviews dated 2026): Exigo, ByDesign, Infinite MLM and Epixel. DirectScale's site is live but undated. The brief-derived target of "10 verified active" is **not met at the strong standard** (§3.3).
3. **Pricing is hidden by six of seven vendors that were captured.** Only Infinite MLM displays prices on its own site (one-time licence: Basic $699, Standard $5,399, S-096). Directory listings add Exigo "Basic" $5,000 per month flat, ByDesign "Freedom" from $2,500, MarketPowerPRO from $499 per feature (S-097, S-098, S-099). Prices differ by an order of magnitude and are in different units (see 02 §5.2).
4. **The enterprise tier has consolidated.** DirectScale's own homepage now reads "DirectScale is now an Exigo company" (S-090) [VF, C-grade, consistent with the 2022 acquisition reports S-061]. Ownership of ByDesign beyond its own statements is unverified.
5. **Payout architecture splits into two camps [IP].** Enterprise vendors integrate third-party payment and payout providers (Exigo lists FlexPay, Global Access, Nexio, PayQuicker and Worldpay; InfoTrax shows Nexio, Avalara and Hyperwallet logos; DirectScale authorizes commissions through a customer-integrated provider). Low-end vendors advertise **in-software wallets, e-pins and crypto** (Epixel: 15+ cryptocurrency wallets; Infinite: e-wallets, e-pins, crypto payment option). This bears directly on the R-39 design stance (51 §5).
6. **Directory review text points to cost-of-change and clarity problems, not commission errors [IP, weak].** Recurring complaint themes in the four review pages read: paid customization, change requests handled through tickets, thin documentation, surprise charges after contract, requirement mis-specification, reporting that is "cumbersome", and a learning curve. Commission-calculation accuracy was **not** a complaint theme in the reviews visible (pros often praise configurable commission engines). The brief assumes commission errors are a major pain; **this is not supported by these reviews and needs primary research (V-02)**.
7. **Nothing here proves a gap.** Reviews are a small, selected sample with vendor replies; customer counts are vendor claims; feature absence cannot be inferred from silence.

### 4.1 Exigo (V2p; S-091, S-097, S-114)

- **Positioning [vendor claim]:** commissions, genealogy, mobile-first back office, e-commerce, CRM, "Exigo Insights", "Exigo AI", "Exigo Engage" app; claims 100+ brands and "$6 billion in annual transactions"; claims SOC 2, GDPR and PCI DSS.

- **Payments:** lists payment partners FlexPay, Global Access, Nexio, PayQuicker and Worldpay. PayQuicker is described by Exigo as "a global payouts orchestration solution" reaching "over 210 countries and territories" via debit cards, mobile wallets and bank transfer (S-114, 18 Nov 2024). **The page does not say who holds funds** and makes no licensing claim.
- **Price [Grade C, directory]:** "Basic" **$5,000 per month, flat**; listing updated 29 Sep 2026; 4.5/5 from 20 reviews (S-097). Some reviews are labelled "Direct Scale".
- **Reviews:** praise for a configurable commission engine and responsive support; complaints that the product is "not intuitive", documentation is thin and customization costs extra (S-097).
