## How to read this register

- **Grade** follows the Master Plan §5.3: **A** primary/official with visible method; **B** credible secondary; **C** vendor marketing, directory listings, blogs, anecdote.
- **Read depth** is added here because it matters for trust:
  - **Full** = the whole page or document was fetched and read.
  - **Excerpt** = only the text excerpt returned by a search tool was read. A claim supported only by an excerpt is real but has **not** been checked against the full page.
- **Date** is the publication date shown on the source; `n/d` = not determined (not guessed).
- Unverified items are logged in the "Recapture and gaps" list at the end. Nothing below is a claim about the sources beyond what is stated in "Claims supported".

## Source-quality observations

1. **53 of 125 entries were read in full** (8 in Wave 1; 2 Wave 1 entries upgraded and 24 new in Wave 2; 19 of the 23 new Wave 3 entries, as targeted page reads). The other 72 are excerpts, including both 10-Ks. Legal findings in 51 remain **screening-grade**, not advice-grade: Malaysia's Act 500 and India's notified Rules have still not been read as primary text, and counsel must review before any design decision depends on them.
2. **Only one A-grade market data source exists for global figures** (WFDSA, S-001), and it is industry-run with estimates. The US figure is independently published by DSA/DSEF (S-003 to S-005), but both are the same industry ecosystem.
3. **"Best MLM software" content is dominated by vendors** (Grade C, self-interested). No independent analyst report on the MLM-software category has been found yet; paywalled research (IBISWorld, Euromonitor and similar) is not accessible here and is not cited.
4. **Directory review counts are small** (at most 71 per vendor), so they are weak evidence on satisfaction or complaints.
