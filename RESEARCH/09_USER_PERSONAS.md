## 0. Summary

1. **There are no research-based personas yet.** A persona based on research means a person was heard. **No interview, survey or community post from any user was obtained** (the Reddit search failed and was not retried). What exists is:
   - **One document-based role profile** (PR-01, Commission Systems Analyst): the role's duties, tools and skills come from three employer postings (S-130, S-131, S-132). Goals, pain and motivation are still hypotheses.
   - **Six provisional hypotheses** (PR-02 to PR-07) built from filings, regulator documents, one consumer case and the brief.
2. **No names, ages, photos, quotes, family details or invented backstories are used.** The brief asks to avoid stereotypical or invented personas, and any such detail would be invented. Each persona is a **role profile**.
3. **Every field is tagged.** A field with no tag in the table is a hypothesis. Fields that rest on a source name it.
4. **One distributor persona is not enough, and cannot be split yet.** Young Living's own statement shows a very wide spread (highest rank Associate: average $22, median $0; Senior Star: average $1,427; S-140), and an academic study found that reasons for joining are often replaced by other reasons for staying (S-135). Segments of distributors must come from interviews, not from invention.
5. **Each persona names the interview that would convert it** to research-based (66 Appendix A).
