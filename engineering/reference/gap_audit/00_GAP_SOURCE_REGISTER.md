## Source-quality notes

1. **Fourteen of 35 entries are Grade A**, mostly first-party documents (an FTC order and notice, payment-provider policies, companies' own compensation plans, a vendor's own help centre). These are stronger than most of the original register's evidence on the same questions.
2. **Company compensation plans are read as documents of their own rules**, which is what they are. They are not evidence of how the plan performs or whether it is lawful.
3. **Vendor help-centre articles are evidence that a feature is documented**, not that it works well. They are still stronger than marketing-page silence, which the original research relied on.
4. **Tax and data-protection summaries are from law and accounting firms (Grade B).** They are dated; primary texts and local advisers must confirm them before any design depends on them.
