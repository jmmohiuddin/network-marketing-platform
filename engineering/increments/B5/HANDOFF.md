## 3. Contracts later increments rely on
- **Decisions:** D-B5-01..24 (PLAN §0); E-ADR-48 in `ARCHITECTURE_DECISION_LOG.md` points at PLAN §0 (written by the records step).
- **Portal realm:** a portal transaction = `withTenant({tenantId, userId, participantId})` (sets `app.participant_id`); every staff route uses `inTenant`, which refuses portal callers; portal routes use `requirePortal` + `inPortal`. Any new staff route is covered by T-B5-40 (portal session on every non-portal route → 401/403/404); any new table holding one participant's data needs a `portal_self` policy (or `portal_hidden`) and a cross-participant assertion.
- **Re-auth:** sensitive portal changes (bank, tax, erasure, TOTP enrolment) need a fresh step-up from `POST /v1/auth/sessions/current/reauth` (password) or TOTP; the web opens `PasswordStepUpModal` on STEP_UP_REQUIRED in the portal realm.
- **Notifications:** written only by `notify_participant` / `notify_run_statements` / `notify_batch` / `notify_plan_change` inside the business transaction; params carry codes and masked values, never staff text or hold reasons. PAY calls `notify_batch` after the batch's instructions exist.
- **Cases:** a dispute is opened only by the line's payee in a portal transaction (trace copied, one open per line; `POST /v1/cases` refuses it); in a portal transaction case_event accepts only opened/submitted/withdrawn by the acting user; `escalated` only with no actor on an overdue submitted dispute; `assigned_to` must be a staff account. Effect previews store participant refs as `{id, external_ref, display_name: null}`.
- **Privacy:** the privacy case and its `privacy_request` are inserted in one transaction (checked at COMMIT); contact_match verifies access/portability only; erasure blockers `open_payable`, `payout_in_flight`, `hold`, `open_run` → 409 ERASURE_DEFERRED; `ERASURE_CLEARED` (TS) equals the SQL cleared list; ledger, runs, relationships and snapshots are kept so committed runs replay identically.
- **Bank details:** compute `cooling_off_until` in SQL (`now() + make_interval(hours => h)`); open the SHARED_ACCOUNT case before any version; a self change is refused while any bank_detail case of the payee is open or submitted. A portal account's email/idp_subject change only through erasure; a deactivated portal account is frozen.
- **Settings (no defaults):** `disputes.policy`, `privacy.policy` (DPO approves), `portal.customer_contact_visible` (DPO approves); unset = the rule is absent and the screen says so.
