## 3. Contracts later increments rely on
- **Decisions:** D-B6-01..18 (PLAN §0, §A); E-ADR-49 in `ARCHITECTURE_DECISION_LOG.md` points at them (written by the records step).
- **Plan governance:** a filing applies only to the content hash it was recorded for; the notice is recorded once, in the transaction that moves the version approved → scheduled (GUC `app.scheduled_plan_version`, survives RELEASE SAVEPOINT, also after activation in that transaction).
- **Period close:** `period.close` + step-up, single sign-off. Take the market ledger lock before `createJournalExport`. Blockers: late_item_pending, payout_exception, payout_in_transit, restatement_open, returned_payment_case, payout_batch_open (422 PERIOD_CLOSE_BLOCKED). The GL export must cover the period (debit total equal; NULL only with no posted debits), enforced in `period_state_guard`. Any posting into a closed period gets 23514 DETAIL `period_closed` (→ 409 PERIOD_CLOSED); `led_attribution_period` returns committed/paid periods only (NULL → 422 NO_COMMITTED_PERIOD).
- **Full export:** never write audit rows from a REPEATABLE READ transaction. Read in an inner READ ONLY RR transaction and write in an outer READ COMMITTED one. File rows of failed or expired exports may be deleted; erasure expires completed exports and fails running ones with an audit. `EXPORT_TABLE_MAX_BYTES` 128 MiB; job `expire_in` 4 h.
- **Migration:** `mig_apply_batch` moves the migration to applied itself (callers must not update the state); refusals MG001 with JSON findings; transient SQLSTATEs (40001, 40P01, 55P03, 57014) propagate and are retryable. The opening needs a `migration` approval from another person; sign-off needs a second, different `migration` approval from neither the creator nor a classifier; accepting a difference needs a `parallel_run_difference` approval from someone other than its classifier. Runs of the cutover period are blocked until sign-off. Opening-state columns are JSON arrays (`'[]'`), shape equal to `qualification_state` incl. `grace_used`.
- **Lists:** saved views and CSV exports are a registry (orders, ledger entries); exports are audited (`EXPORT.CREATED` with filters, sort, columns); vendor/support sessions never export (403).
- **Settings (no defaults):** `plan.filing_requirements` (approver `market.signoff`), `exports.full_schedule` (step-up); `orders.crediting_event` retired: plan versions carry the crediting event and placement width; no plan → 422 CREDITING_EVENT_NOT_CONFIGURED.
