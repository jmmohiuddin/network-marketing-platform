| D-V1A-02 | **Provisional runs (FR-COMP-011, BR-PER-07): a calendar declares itself provisional for one final calendar.** `calendar.provisional_for_calendar_id` (set at creation only, same market, the target is not itself provisional, no chains). A provisional calendar's periods are ordinary periods: their runs use the plan rules scoped to that calendar (the "provisional components"), commit with the usual second person and step-up, post to the ledger and pay. **Provisional periods nest in final periods** (C-V1A-17): a provisional calendar's periods are generated on its own grid (`first_period_start + k × step`) **cut at the final calendar's period boundaries** (a step that crosses a boundary becomes two periods, e.g. weeks Oct 26–Nov 1 and Nov 1–Nov 2), generation stops at the final calendar's last generated period, and the SQL period guard refuses a provisional period that does not lie inside one final period. A provisional period is **trued up by the final period that contains it** (`final.starts_at ≤ prov.starts_at AND prov.ends_at ≤ final.ends_at`, SQL `provisional_final_period`), so every order an advance paid on is credited to the final period that deducts the advance and no volume is paid twice in a month. The final run's snapshot carries one **adjustment origin of type `provisional`** per (committed provisional run of those periods, payee position, rule key) with `amount = −Σ amount_minor` of that run's result lines for the pair (zero sums skipped); the engine consumes them in pass 9 like late adjustments, so the final run's lines = final amounts + negative advance lines and **true-up per payee = final − provisional = Σ of the final run's lines for the payee** (validation). A negative true-up is netted by the existing engine/B4 rules (payable offset first, rest receivable = the clawback policy). Late items, order-line clawbacks and restatements are **not** generated for provisional calendars (the final run includes every order credited in its period): COMP skips them; a restatement request on a provisional run → 422 PROVISIONAL_RUN. Preconditions: final run creation → 409 PROVISIONAL_RUN_OPEN `{period_ids}` while a mapped provisional period has a queued/running/calculated run; provisional run creation or commit → 409 FINAL_PERIOD_COMMITTED once the final period has a committed run (SQL backstop); final commit re-checks the offsets under the market periods lock → 409 RUN_SUPERSEDED `{reason: 'provisional_changed'}`; both calendars must belong to one plan (422 PROVISIONAL_PLAN_MISMATCH); a provisional calendar's rules must not pay in credits (422 PROVISIONAL_CREDIT_RULE, credits are not offset). | Reuses the B3 adjustment-origin path (no new posting logic, replay identical); the spec says "a calendar can declare provisional payments" and "final run … posts the difference". |
| D-V1A-03 | **Non-cash rewards (FR-FIN-006, BR-CRD-01/02).** `credit_type` (tenant-wide) has code (immutable), name, `unit_value_minor` (optional display unit), `expiry_days` + `expiry_notice_days` (both or neither; no default), `redemption_scope` (`{markets?, item_classes?}`, null = any), `taxable` (required, no default) and `active`. A pay rule of a plan version may name `credit_type_code`: its lines are **credit awards**, valued in the market currency's minor units. The engine routes award-rule lines out of cash settlement (not netted against receivables, not withheld, not subject to minimum payout) into `creditAwards`; COMP writes `credit_award` rows (copying expiry days, notice days, taxable and unit value from the type at calculation) in the run-output transaction; result lines, run totals and `run_settlement` stay cash only. Postings (MONEY "not specified", C-V1A-6): **award** in the run entry (`led_post_run`): Dr `commission_expense` / Cr `credit_liability` (payee × type); **redemption** (event `credit_redeemed`, origin `credit_redemption`, key `credit-redeem:<redemption_id>`): Dr `credit_liability` / Cr `settled_clearing` (settled in kind); **expiry** (event `credit_expired`, origin `credit_award`, key `credit-expire:<award_id>`): Dr `credit_liability` / Cr `breakage`; both without approval, posted through `ledger_post` and attributed to `led_attribution_period(market)` (none → 422 NO_COMMITTED_PERIOD, the PAY/write-off code; the cron skips and retries). A committed award is a **lot** expiring at the end of `committed_at` date (market zone) + `expiry_days`; consumption is FIFO by (expires_on NULLS LAST, committed_at, award id); a redemption first expires the payee's due lots, then refuses an amount above the unexpired remainder (422 CREDIT_EXPIRED when the balance covers it only with expired lots, else 422 CREDIT_INSUFFICIENT). ## §2 API contract
Conventions: `/v1`, envelope `{data, meta}` / `{error: {code, message, details}}`, cursor pagination (`cursor`, `limit` ≤ 50, `meta.next_cursor`), zod strict, money as decimal strings, rates as 10-dp strings, instants ISO-8601 UTC `Z` with ms, dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, Idempotency-Key on creating POSTs, step-up = TOTP window (`STEP_UP_REQUIRED` 401). Staff routes: `requireRealm('tenant')` + `requirePermission`, `inTenant`, market scope per D-V1A-09. Portal routes: `requirePortal`, `inPortal`. Vendor routes: vendor realm (`modules/ops` pattern). Every write route that resolves a tenant (session, key, connector or provider webhook, public slug) is subject to 409 TENANT_READ_ONLY (§2.5).

- Proration of a provisional step across final periods: not needed, the step is cut at the final boundary (D-V1A-02, C-V1A-17); a provisional cadence other than weekly/monthly.

- Automated purge without the operator CLI; purge of tenant files outside `UPLOAD_DIR`.

- TK-BM-16 remaining shortcuts; product confirmations (D-B6-18, IN pack C-9, tax data in subject export, E-ADR-51 budget, AC-S1-2 amendment if written).
