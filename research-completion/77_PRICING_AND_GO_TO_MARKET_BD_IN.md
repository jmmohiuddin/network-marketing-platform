## 2. Proposed price structure (to test, not to publish)

| Edition / offer | Structure | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **MVP-A Compliance Analytics (hosted)** | Flat monthly subscription by company size band; first report free as a diagnostic | Simple to buy; does not touch payouts; tests WTP directly (76 Model 2) |
| **Engine + compliance (hosted, MVP-B)** | Per active payee with a monthly minimum + fixed-scope implementation (DEC-07); **never a share of sales volume** | Tracks cost to serve; does not reward recruitment |
| **Self-hosted (V1)** | Annual licence by payee band, including updates to the India pack (OQ-24) | Answers buyers who require their own servers; the India data rule may push some buyers here |
| What not to offer | One-time perpetual licence at local price points; e-wallet or e-pin modules | Cannot fund market-pack maintenance; DEC-03 |

**Test points for the demand test (FD-10):** a design partner is asked to accept one of ₹30,000 / ₹60,000 / ₹1,00,000 per month for MVP-A after seeing its own R1–R5 reports. The pass rule is set **before** the conversation.

## 3. Go-to-market (India)

| Step | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | List Indian direct-selling entities from IDSA membership and public seller-register pages (the Rules require each entity to publish one) | No company count exists (71 §4); the registers themselves are a lead source |
| 2 | Segment by size (active sellers) and category (wellness 60%, cosmetics 26%) | Viability needs larger tenants (76) |
| 3 | Lead with a **free compliance diagnostic**: the company uploads one period's export, gets R1–R5 and a grievance/register checklist against the Rules | Compliance is the only differentiated value (73); low effort for the buyer |
| 4 | Sell to the Chief Compliance Officer and the CFO, not to the field | The Rules create the CCO role (71); the buyer is the company (A-01) |
| 5 | Channel partners: chartered-accountant firms and direct-selling law practices | They already advise on the Rules and s.393; credible introducers |
| 6 | Content: plain explanations of the Rules, s.393 withholding and DPDP timelines for direct sellers | Search demand for compliance topics; no competitor publishes it (73) |
| 7 | Avoid: income claims, recruitment-growth messaging, "MLM software" keyword marketing | Brand risk; DEC-05 |

**Growth loop (hypothesis):** each tenant publishes a seller register and earnings-related disclosures generated by the product; other companies' compliance officers see the format. Untested.

## 5. Risks to this plan

| Risk | Effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Indian buyers see compliance as a lawyer's job, not software | No sale for MVP-A | Diagnostic shows effort saved; partner with law firms |
| Price anchor of $699 one-time | Low acceptance of subscriptions | Sell to larger companies; tie price to compliance outputs |
| Rules change (new Direct Selling Act) | Pack rework | Versioned pack; subscription funds updates |
| Reputational association with MLM | Partners and providers decline | Honesty policy; refuse unregistered entities (guardrails) |
