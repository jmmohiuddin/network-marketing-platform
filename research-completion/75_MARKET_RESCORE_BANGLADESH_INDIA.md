## 3. Reading

1. **India ranks first of the two in every case.** Even India's low case (2.60) beats Bangladesh-affiliate's base case (2.35).
2. **Bangladesh is a "do not enter" for MLM operators** — the same conclusion as research 02 §6, now with 2025 enforcement evidence.
3. **Bangladesh-affiliate overlaps India only in its high case**, and that case requires a favourable counsel opinion, a payout route and buyers that may not exist. It is a **watch** item, not a launch market.
4. India's score is **moderate, not strong**: the weak cells are price (2) and competition (2). India is viable only for a **compliance-led** offer.
5. Comparison with earlier candidates (US, Malaysia) is **not possible on the same basis**: they were never scored numerically. Research 02 §6 placed US, Malaysia and India together as "Candidate".
