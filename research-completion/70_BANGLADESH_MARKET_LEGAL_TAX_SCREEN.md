## 0. Result in five lines

1. **Multi-level marketing is closed in practice.** The 2013 Act requires a licence; four were issued in 2014, none renewed after 2015, none issued since. Operating without one carries 5–10 years' imprisonment and a fine up to Tk 50 lakh.
2. **Enforcement is active and recent:** e-commerce MLM sites shut (2022); Bangladesh Bank circular (March 2025) treats MLM, pyramid and Ponzi schemes as money-laundering risks; accounts frozen.
3. **Selling an MLM engine to Bangladeshi operators would mean selling a tool for an activity no one is licensed to run.** Recommendation: **do not target Bangladeshi MLM operators** (unchanged from research 51 §3.2, now with 2025 evidence).
4. **What might remain:** single-level affiliate or reseller commission programmes run by e-commerce or retail companies. Whether such a programme falls outside the 2013 Act's definition is **a question for counsel**, not a conclusion here.
5. **Data:** a new personal-data law exists (2025, amended 2026); sources disagree on how strict its localisation rule is. Treat as open.

## 3. Data protection

| Item | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Law | Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 (Ordinance No. 61 of 2025), gazetted Nov 2025 | P2-S09, P2-S10 |
| Localisation (strict reading) | "Confidential" and "restricted" personal data to be stored in Bangladesh; 18-month grace to about May 2027 | P2-S09 (bd-scl, Apr 2026) |
| Localisation (narrow reading) | Government statement (25 Oct 2025): **no blanket localisation**; "cloud-first policy"; restriction only for a narrow restricted category (NID, biometrics, voter rolls, date of birth, core banking identifiers); overseas processing allowed with accountability to Bangladeshi courts | P2-S11 (Financial Express) |
| Amendment | Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Ordinance 2026, No. 23, promulgated 5 Feb 2026, concerning data residency | P2-S12 (Digital Policy Alert; details behind sign-in) |
| Later law | One secondary source reports replacement by a Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (April 2026) with fines up to Tk 25–50 lakh and a Chief Data Officer duty, and names the regulator inconsistently | P2-S10 (Securiti) — **unconfirmed** |
| Fines | 1–2% of turnover; 2–5% for significant data fiduciaries (Ordinance version) | P2-S09 |

**Conflict C-BD-1:** strict versus narrow localisation, and Ordinance versus Act. **Open; counsel (AQ-BD-06).** Product consequence if strict: Bangladeshi personal data in a Bangladeshi data centre; no hyperscaler region exists in Bangladesh (78).

## 5. Implications

| For | Implication |
|---|---|
| Market packs | **No Bangladesh MLM pack.** A Bangladesh "affiliate" pack is possible only after AQ-BD-01; it would block multi-level overrides at configuration (a guardrail outcome **Blocked** for any rule paying on a recruit's recruits) |
| Positioning | If the founder is Bangladesh-based, Bangladesh can be the **build location**, not the **market** |
| Guardrails | Existing guardrail model can express "single-level only" as a market-level block; no new mechanism needed |
| Payments | See 72: bKash corporate disbursement exists; eligibility for commission programmes unknown |
