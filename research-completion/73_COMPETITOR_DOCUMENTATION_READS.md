**Reading.**
1. **Price anchor is very low.** Buyers in India can obtain a working MLM back office for a one-time $699–$5,399, or a custom build for $3,000–$15,000.
2. **The local offer centres on e-wallets and e-pins** — exactly what DEC-03 excludes, and what Indian payment and Bangladeshi enforcement sources associate with risk.
3. **No local vendor read states India-specific compliance features** (grievance register with timers, seller register publication, s.393 withholding, purchase-qualification reports). This is the same gap as internationally, now confirmed for two Indian vendors.
4. **Implication:** competing on calculation features against $699 licences is not viable. The only defensible offer in India is **compliance evidence plus correctness** sold to companies that face scrutiny — and the willingness to pay for that is still unmeasured (OQ-01).
