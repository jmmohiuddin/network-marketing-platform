Statutory Accounting Disclosures & Expenses 16 CFR §437.3(a)(4)
receipt_long 1. Independent Contractor Status & Taxes

Earnings shown reflect gross amounts paid by Acrobex Global and estimated retail markup before deduction of expenses. Participants are independent contractors, not employees. Participants are exclusively responsible for all applicable self-employment, state, and federal taxes.

point_of_sale 2. Operating Expenses & Inventory Overhead

Figures do NOT account for operating expenses incurred by participants, which may include starter kit acquisition ($49.00 USD), optional marketing materials, convention attendance, telecommunications, travel, and promotional shipping costs.

replay 3. 90% Buyback Protection Guarantee

Pursuant to the DSA Code of Ethics, Acrobex provides an explicit 90% inventory repurchase guarantee on unopened, resalable inventory purchased within the preceding 12 calendar months upon participant account cancellation.

balance 4. Prohibited Compensation Claims

Distributors are prohibited under company bylaws and Federal Trade Commission rules from making lifestyle, guaranteed income, medical health cure claims, or displaying speculative earnings testimonials during recruitment.
